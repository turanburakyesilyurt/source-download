Source Download is ready

Three ways to capture what you need from any web page.

  1. 1

    Right-click on any page

    Full-page and area screenshots, area recording, colour picker and an offline copy — right where you are.

  2. 2

    Or use the toolbar button

    Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin Source Download, so the tools are always one click away.

  3. 3

    Press F12 for everything on the page

    Choose the Source Download tab in DevTools. Every image, video, font and file the page loaded is listed there — preview it, filter it and save it as a ZIP. Page tables export to Excel.

Everything runs on your computer. No account, no tracking, nothing is sent to us.
