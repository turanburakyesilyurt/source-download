How to use the full panel

  1. 1
    Open DevTools for the page you want to inspect
    F12 or Ctrl+Shift+I (macOS: ⌥+⌘+I)
  2. 2 Click the Source Download tab in the DevTools toolbar
  3. 3 Browse, inspect and download resources — individually or all at once as a ZIP.

Note: Chrome does not allow extensions to programmatically open DevTools, which is why the full panel is reached through the DevTools toolbar.
